## Manual review summary

Human review of 30 sampled factual claims (3 per product): 26 valid, 4 invalid, 0 hallucinations. All 4 invalid marks were pricing_comparison claims; 3 of them were 'pricing unavailable' claims for JS-rendered pricing pages (Slack, Notion, Lovable) where the live page does show prices in a browser — the crawler cannot execute JavaScript, so the claims described retrieval truthfully but the citations do not demonstrate the prices' absence.
